How to install in Gmail:
  1. Fill out or update your details.
  2. Click Copy Signature for Gmail.
  3. In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) → See all settings.
  4. Scroll down to Signature, create or select one, and press Ctrl+V.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
